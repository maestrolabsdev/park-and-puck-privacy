# Park & Puck — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 10, 2026
**Developer:** Maestro Dev Labs

Park & Puck is an air hockey game for Android phones and Android Auto.

**Data we collect**

None. Park & Puck does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal or device data.
The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

**Data stored on your device**

The game saves your settings (language, sound, vibration, rink, goals to win, last difficulty and side)
and the state of a paused match locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is removed
when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

**Permissions**

* Vibration: used only for haptic feedback during the game. You can turn it off in Settings.

**Android Auto**

When you play on a parked car's screen through Android Auto, the game runs on your phone. It does not read
vehicle data or location.

**Children**

The game does not collect data from anyone, including children.

**Purchases**

If optional in-app purchases are added in the future, payments will be processed by Google Play under
Google's privacy policy; we will update this page before that release.

**Changes**

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date.
