# Park & Puck — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 11, 2026
**Developer:** Maestro Dev Labs

Park & Puck is an air hockey game for Android phones and Android Auto.

## Data we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data.
The app has no accounts of its own, no analytics and no advertising.

## Google Play Games

The game uses Google Play Games Services for achievements and the leaderboard. Sign-in, your Play Games
profile, achievements and scores are handled by Google under the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not receive your email address or other
personal data.

## Data stored on your device

The game saves your settings (language, sound, vibration, rink, goals to win, difficulty and side),
the state of a paused match, your achievement progress and your Career Points total locally on your device.
This data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Permissions

* Internet: used only by Google Play Games Services.
* Vibration: used only for haptic feedback during the game. You can turn it off in Settings.

## Android Auto

When you play on a parked car's screen through Android Auto, the game runs on your phone. It does not read
vehicle data or location.

## Children

The game does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## Purchases

If optional in-app purchases are added in the future, payments will be processed by Google Play under
Google's privacy policy; we will update this page before that release.

## Deleting your data

Park & Puck by Maestro Dev Labs does not keep any of your data on our servers, so there is nothing for us to
delete or retain.

* Data on your device (settings, paused match, achievement progress, Career Points total) is deleted when you
  uninstall the app or clear its data in Android Settings > Apps > Park & Puck > Storage.
* Google Play Games data (your Play Games profile, achievements and leaderboard scores) is stored by Google.
  Delete it in the Google Play Games app (Profile > Settings > Delete Play Games account & data) or in your
  [Google Account](https://myaccount.google.com/). Retention of that data is governed by the Google Privacy
  Policy.

## Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date.
